# Code of Conduct

This project adopts a short-form adaptation of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

Be respectful. Disagree on technical merit, not on people. Assume good faith. If you find yourself getting heated, walk away from the keyboard for 20 minutes.

## Expected behaviour

- Use welcoming, inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.
- Anything a reasonable professional would recognise as out of place in a working codebase.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when an individual is officially representing the project (e.g. using a project email address or speaking as a maintainer).

## Enforcement

Report violations to `conduct@tryarkon.dev`. Reports will be reviewed and acted on by the maintainers within a reasonable timeframe. Confidentiality of the reporter will be respected.

Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including warnings, temporary bans, or permanent bans from the project's spaces.
